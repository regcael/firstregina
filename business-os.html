Frequently Asked Questions

Why build on a Free Plan instead of paid project management tools?

Most small teams do not need expensive monthly SaaS subscriptions like Asana or Monday.com. Your system is built on a Free Plan, so you don't need to add another monthly software subscription. You can upgrade to a paid plan if you want to.

What if my team isn't tech-savvy?

The system is deliberately built for low friction. Your team won't navigate backend databases—they get a simple, personalized daily dashboard showing only the tasks assigned to them today. The short explainer videos provided at handover ensure every team member knows exactly where to click.

How much time will this require from me during the build?

Less than 45 minutes. You fill out a structured intake form outlining your team roles, recurring projects, and pain points. We handle the architecture, layout, and logic from there.

PS: if you don't want to fill the form, we can get on a call 🤙

Do I get a revision?

One focused revision round is included. After you review the completed system, you can request adjustments to layouts, property labels, filters and sorting within the agreed architecture.

Need something beyond that?

Additional features or structural changes can be quoted separately. Prepaid and booked in advance.

Is 5-Day delivery possible?

Yes—as long as we get the information, access, and approvals we need when we need them. Your 5-working-day build window starts once we receive your advance payment and completed intake form. If you delay sending information or access, we pause the build window and may assign you to the next available build slot.
